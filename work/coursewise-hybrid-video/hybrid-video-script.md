# Coursewise Hybrid Product Video Script

Total video length: 3:35

## 0:00–0:18 — Introduction

Hi everyone. For this project, I designed Coursewise, a mobile competitor to Canvas Student. It helps students understand their grades and feedback, keep track of deadlines, and plan when to do their work. I wanted the mobile experience to be useful for more than just checking information.

## 0:18–0:43 — User need

I interviewed four students about how they currently use Canvas. Most of them mainly open the mobile app to check grades and deadlines. When they need to read detailed feedback or act on it, they usually move back to their laptop. They also use tools like Google Calendar or a separate to-do list because Canvas does not give them enough planning support.

## 0:43–1:03 — Opportunity

This showed me that the problem was bigger than confusing navigation. A grade could appear without enough context. Feedback was difficult to locate. And seeing a deadline did not help students decide when they should start working. Coursewise connects these pieces and gives students a useful next step.

## 1:03–1:23 — Prototype: Today

This is the main Today page. It brings the most important updates together, including new grades and assignments that are due soon. Each item shows the course code and course name, so students can understand the context quickly without opening several different course pages.

## 1:23–1:48 — Prototype: Grades and feedback

When a student opens a new grade, Coursewise shows the score and explains how it affects the overall course grade. In the Grades section, students can see the score breakdown, an AI summary, and the instructor's actual feedback together. This directly addresses the problem students described during my interviews, where feedback was difficult to find.

## 1:48–2:18 — Prototype: Scheduling work

The second main feature turns a deadline into a realistic work plan. Coursewise suggests work sessions before the assignment is due and explains why it selected that time. Students can use the suggestion or choose their own date, start time, duration, and calendar. This gives them support without taking away their control.

## 2:18–2:33 — Prototype: Confirmation

After the student saves the session, the confirmation repeats the task, date, time, duration, and calendar destination. This makes it clear that the action worked and tells the student exactly what was added.

## 2:33–2:58 — Prototype: Plan

The saved session then appears in the Plan tab next to the original assignment deadline. Coursewise clearly labels planned work and due dates as different types of items. Students can open a planned session to review it, change the time, or remove it without deleting the original assignment.

## 2:58–3:23 — Testing

I tested the prototype with four students, and ninety percent of the tasks were completed without help. The testing still showed me where the design needed improvement. I made the path to feedback clearer, strengthened the saved-session confirmation, explained the suggested time, and made deadlines more visually different from planned work.

## 3:23–3:35 — Conclusion

The final result connects grades, feedback, deadlines, and planning in one mobile experience. Coursewise helps students understand what happened and decide what to do next. The full interactive prototype is available at the link shown here.
